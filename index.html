AI KPI Analyst

LLM Analytics

A tool that looks at a KPI change and works out what caused it. It tests explanations with its own SQL queries, then checks every number in its answer against the query results before showing it.

  • True cause in top 3 in 7 of 12 tests (vs 1 of 12 baseline)
  • Unsupported claims cut from 29.8% to 0% with a verifier

Question

When a KPI changes, what actually caused it — and can an LLM explain it without guessing?

Data

99K e-commerce orders, broken down by category, region, seller, and payment type.

Method

Revenue is split into volume vs. order value, and shift-share separates rate effects from mix effects. An LLM agent tests each explanation with read-only SQL, and a verifier checks every figure it cites against the query results.

Result

True cause in top 3 in 7 of 12 planted problems (baseline: 1 of 12). Unsupported numbers fell from 29.8% to 1.5%, and to 0% with the verifier on.

Python · SQL · DuckDB · Ollama · Streamlit

Walmart Demand Forecasting

Forecasting

A demand forecast built on Walmart sales data. It covers 9,147 product–store combinations across three Texas stores, with the inventory cost worked out next to each forecast.

  • 17.8M rows of demand data analyzed
  • 9,147 product–store series forecast

Question

What will demand look like across thousands of product–store combinations — and what does the inventory cost?

Data

17.8M rows of demand data; 9,147 product–store series; 3 stores across Texas.

Method

Python and DuckDB SQL handle the data, LightGBM does the forecasting, and the results go into Power BI.

Result

Forecasts and inventory cost analysis for all 9,147 series, delivered in Power BI for inventory decisions.

Python · DuckDB SQL · LightGBM · Power BI
